## Day 3: What is Musharaka?
**Image:** `post3-what-is-musharaka.png`

```
Why did we name this community Musharaka? 🤔
لماذا اخترنا اسم "مشاركة"؟

Musharaka (مشاركة) is the Islamic model of business partnership:

💰 Partners contribute capital, effort, or both
🤝 Profit is shared according to a ratio agreed upfront
⚖️ Losses are shared according to each partner's share of the capital
🚫 No interest (riba): risk and reward are shared fairly

It's built on trust, transparency, and fairness, which is exactly the kind of partnership we want every member here to build.

المشاركة: شراكة يساهم فيها كل طرف بالمال أو الجهد أو كليهما، ويُقسم الربح بحسب الاتفاق، وتُوزّع الخسارة بقدر رأس مال كل شريك. شراكة قائمة على الثقة والعدل، بلا ربا.

💬 Have you ever been in a business partnership? What's the #1 lesson you learned?
هل دخلت في شراكة تجارية من قبل؟ ما أهم درس تعلمته؟

Note: this is a general overview, not a fatwa. For your specific agreement, consult a qualified scholar and a lawyer.
```
