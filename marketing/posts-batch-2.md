# Musharaka Collective: Post Batch 2 (based on trend research)

These use the formats that perform best on "فكرة مشروع" (see `research/inspiration.md`), each with a **partnership twist** so comments turn into connections.
Post 1 a day. Text-only posts are fine; plain one-line questions often get more comments than designed images.

---

### 1. Skill question (high comments, and it's matching data)
```
🟡 What skill or craft are you really good at? 👇
Someone in this group may be looking for exactly you.

🟡 ما هي المهارة أو الحرفة التي تتقنها جيداً؟ 👇
ربما يبحث عنك أحد الأعضاء هنا.
```

### 2. Multiple income (high likes)
```
🔴 Don't rely only on your job. Build a second income, and build it with the right partner. 🤝

🔴 لا تعتمد على الوظيفة فقط… ابنِ مصدر دخل ثانٍ مع الشريك المناسب. 🤝
```

### 3. Profit debate (the top-commented format)
```
🟡 In a 50/50 partnership, one partner brings the money and the other does all the work.
Is a 50/50 profit split fair? 🤔

🟡 في شراكة: شريك يدفع المال، والآخر يقوم بكل العمل.
هل تقسيم الربح 50/50 عادل؟ 🤔
```
*Follow-up post 2 days later: explain how musharaka and mudaraba handle this (profit ratio is agreed freely; losses follow capital).*

### 4. Field preference
```
🟡 If you started a business tomorrow, which field?
🏭 Manufacturing
🌾 Food / Agriculture
🛒 Trade / E-commerce
💻 Tech / Services
🏠 Real estate

🟡 لو بدأت مشروعاً غداً، أي مجال تختار؟
🏭 صناعة · 🌾 غذاء/زراعة · 🛒 تجارة · 💻 تقنية/خدمات · 🏠 عقارات
```
*Tip: post this as a Facebook **Poll**.*

### 5. "Who wants to build this?" (turns an idea into a partner thread)
```
🔴 Halal meal prep for busy Muslim families is one of the fastest-growing halal businesses in the US. 🍱

Who wants to build this? Comment:
📍 Your city
🛠️ What you'd bring (cooking, marketing, capital, delivery…)

🔴 تحضير وجبات حلال جاهزة للعائلات المسلمة من أسرع المشاريع نمواً في أمريكا.
من يريد بناء هذا المشروع؟ اكتب مدينتك وماذا تقدّم 👇
```
*Repeat weekly with different ideas: modest fashion brand, halal food truck, 3D printing shop, Islamic kids' education app, halal catering, cleaning company, Arabic tutoring, import/export from the Middle East.*

### 6. Job vs. project mindset
```
🔴 I'm not telling you to quit your job. 💼
Use your evenings and weekends to build something of your own, and find a partner so you're not doing it alone.

🔴 لا أطلب منك ترك وظيفتك 💼
استغل وقت فراغك في بناء مشروعك الخاص… وابحث عن شريك حتى لا تبدأ وحدك.
```

### 7. Halal earning reminder (values post)
```
Seek halal earnings, choose honest partners, put everything in writing, and trust Allah with the results. 🤲

اطلب الكسب الحلال، واختر الشريك الأمين، واكتب كل شيء، وتوكّل على الله. 🤲
```

### 8. Capital question
```
🟡 How much money do you think you need to start your first business? 💰
A) Under $5K

🟡 كم تحتاج برأيك لبدء مشروعك الأول؟ 💰

```

🟡 هل دخلت في شراكة تجارية من قبل؟
