# Where to Find Members, and What to Post There

## How to find groups (inside Facebook, about 10 minutes)
Search bar → type a phrase → **Groups** tab. Join groups with **5,000+ members** that show **posts from the last day or two**.

| Category | Search these phrases |
|---|---|
| Muslim business | `Muslim entrepreneurs` · `Muslim business owners` · `Muslim small business` · `Muslim business network USA` |
| Muslim women | `Muslim women in business` · `Muslim mompreneurs` · `hijabi entrepreneurs` |
| Arab American | `Arab American business` · `Arabs in America` · `العرب في أمريكا` · `عرب أمريكا` · `Arab community [your city]` |

## Posting rules (so you don't get removed or flagged)
1. **Read each group's rules first.** Many ban links or self-promotion. If so, post the question without the link and reply to interested commenters with it.
2. **Comment helpfully on 2–3 posts** before you post your own.
3. **Post in at most 2–3 groups a day**, and never the same text twice in one day. Rotate the posts below.
4. **Lead with value or a question**, and put the link last.
5. **Reply to every comment** within a few hours. Replies push the post higher in the feed.

## Groups reviewed

### تعالو نساعد بعض في امريكا ("Let's help each other in America")
https://www.facebook.com/groups/678496455222146. Reviewed 2026-10-04 (last 24 posts).
- **Audience:** Arabs in the US (mostly Egyptian, Arabic-speaking), many home-based sellers.
- **Content:** almost entirely shared ads (jewelry, clothes, Ramadan decor, real estate, Walmart deals). About 20 posts a day.
- **Engagement:** very low. 23 of 24 posts got **0 likes and 0 comments**.
- **Verdict:** ⚠️ Low value. Posting is allowed, but posts drown in ads. Worth **one** original text post (not a share), then move on. Don't post here repeatedly.
- **Tip:** the members are small-business sellers, so frame the post as "grow your small business with a partner."
