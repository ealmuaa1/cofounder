### What works (patterns to copy)
1. **One-line questions with a 🔴 or 🟡 emoji** get hundreds of comments. They're easy to answer and make people feel heard.
2. **Concrete, physical businesses** (3D printing, goats, CNC wood art, e-bikes) beat abstract advice. People want to see the business.
3. **"Escape the 9–5" and "multiple income" mindset lines** get the most likes and shares.
4. **Debate bait about numbers** ("Is 10% profit good?") gets the most comments of all.
5. **"Mention your country/city"** turns a post into a sign-up list.
6. **Reels for reach, text for comments.** They post both daily.
7. **Halal and values lines** ("Seek halal earnings, spend wisely…") fit the audience.
8. **Hashtags:** #فكرة_مشروع #مشاريع_ناجحة #مشروع_مربح #مشاريع_صغيرة #اكسبلور

### What we do better
They give ideas but never help people **act** on them. Musharaka Collective turns "great idea!" comments into **partnerships**: every idea post ends with "Who wants to build this? Comment your city and what you bring."

## 3. Rules for using other pages' content
- ✅ Copy **formats, topics, and question styles**. Those aren't owned by anyone.
- ✅ Write your own captions and make your own images and videos.
- ❌ Don't download and re-upload their Reels or images. That's copyright infringement, and Facebook lowers the reach of reposted content anyway.
- ❌ Skip off-mission topics like gold prospecting. They get views but bring the wrong audience.
