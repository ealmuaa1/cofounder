**Goal:** go from 5 to 100 members by bringing people in from **outside** the group.
**Before Day 1:** switch the group to **Public** (Group settings → Privacy) and add the group link to your Facebook profile **Intro/bio**.

**Your group:** https://www.facebook.com/groups/1631199898377135/

1. **Post in your group** (the daily post below).
